# Creative-system requirements

A creative direction is a production grammar, not a colorway. Each proposed direction must specify
layout family, scene construction, camera behavior, pacing, hierarchy, and metaphor. At least three
direction signatures must be unique across those fields.

Decompose references into transferable principles: density, hierarchy, negative space, crop,
depth, lighting, material, typography, motion, and emotional effect. Do not copy protected artwork
or confuse a reference with factual source media.

For each direction define:

1. hook frame and first three seconds;
2. narrative beats and proof order;
3. scene archetypes and composition grids;
4. typography scale and safe-zone behavior;
5. camera/lens/spatial grammar;
6. movement cadence and one transition motif;
7. material, texture, lighting, and color behavior;
8. audio signature and silence strategy;
9. CTA/endcard system;
10. anti-reference and slop risks.

Reject directions whose only material difference is palette, icon choice, or copy.
