# Brand Voice

## Voice Essence
Reading my writing should feel like standing on a dock at golden hour with someone who knows every cove on the lake. Calm, unhurried, quietly confident. I describe places with sensory specifics instead of adjectives, and I never sound like I'm selling — I sound like I'm showing you something I love.

## Tone Attributes
1. Serene — no urgency language, no pressure, no exclamation points doing the image's job.
2. Established — the voice of someone who belongs here; understated, never trendy slang.
3. Warm — invitational phrasing over transactional phrasing.
4. Precise — one concrete detail beats three vague adjectives; name the timber portico, the sage cabinetry, the fire bowl.

## Mechanical Rules
- Sentence length: short to medium. One breath each. Vary rhythm.
- Exclamation points: maximum one per post, usually zero.
- Hashtags: none. Keywords live in the prose.
- ALL CAPS: only in designed graphics, never in captions.
- Emoji: rare, and only where it replaces a word cleanly.
- Numbers: only ones substantiated by the listing or a cited source.
- Every caption ends with a soft invitation, not a command.

## Banned Words and Phrases
- dream home / your dream awaits
- nestled in the heart of
- don't miss out / act fast / won't last long
- stunning, breathtaking (show it, don't declare it)
- perfect for families (Fair Housing — describe the property, never the buyer)
- safe neighborhood / best schools
- boasts / features (as verbs)

## Tone by Context
- Listing content: lifestyle first, property second. Open with the feeling, land on one detail.
- Community content: local's knowledge, gently proud.
- Market content: plain-spoken, sourced, never alarmist.

## On-Brand Example
Evenings here end the same way — the fire bowl going, the lake settling flat as glass. The new lakefront craftsman keeps that ritual close: timber portico out front, cedar deck over the water out back. Come see it slowly.

## Off-Brand Example
🔥🔥 STUNNING lakefront dream home alert!! This one won't last — nestled in the heart of lake country with luxury finishes throughout! DM me NOW!

## Quality Test
Read it aloud. If it sounds like a billboard, cut it. If it sounds like a neighbor who happens to know the market cold, ship it.
