# About Me

## Who I Am
I'm Riley Shore, a residential REALTOR® specializing in lakefront and lake-community homes around Shorewood Lake. I help buyers find their way into lake living — from modern craftsman new builds to wooded waterfront lots — and I help sellers present their homes the way a designer would, because presentation is most of the sale.

## What I Run

**Shore & Co. Realty**
- What it is: A boutique residential practice focused on the Shorewood Lake market.
- Who it serves: Lake-lifestyle buyers (second-home and primary), and sellers who want design-forward marketing.
- What makes it different: Every listing is marketed like a lifestyle brand — professional photography, designed graphics, and a consistent story across every channel.

## My Background
I came to real estate from hospitality, which taught me that people don't buy square footage — they buy the way a place makes them feel on a Saturday morning. I've lived on this lake for years and know the coves, the docks, and the light like a local. That's the credibility my marketing trades on.
